1. Real-Time Speech-to-Speech Accent Translation

How Sanas works, architectural benchmarks, and the hard reality of low-latency speech modulation.

A. How Sanas.ai Operates (The Acoustic Filter Architecture)

Contrary to common perception, Sanas.ai does NOT use a Cascaded (Speech-to-Text ➔ Text-to-Speech) pipeline. Instead, Sanas performs Direct Acoustic Formant Shifting at the frame level (20ms – 50ms chunks):

  • No Text Intermediate: The raw audio waveform is broken into acoustic latents. It never transcribes speech into text, completely avoiding word hallucination or speech-recognition failure.
  • Phonetic Formant Warping: It selectively adjusts specific vowel formants and consonant articulators (e.g., transforming the Indian retroflex /ʈ/ and /ɖ/ into alveolar flaps, and vowel lengthening for American prosody).
  • Timbre Preservation: The speaker's native vocal cords, pitch inflection, natural breathing, and pauses are untouched. The user sounds like themselves, but with General American or British pronunciation.
  • Edge CPU Execution: Uses custom C++/CUDA kernels running in <10% CPU on standard office laptops without needing an external GPU.
Sanas Supported Accent Matrix:
• Target Output Accents: General American (US English), British Received Pronunciation (UK English), Australian English.
• Source Input Accents: Indian English (all sub-regions), Filipino English, Latin American English, African English, Middle Eastern English.
• Controls: Real-time Accent Intensity Slider (0% to 100%) and Speech Enhancement/Denoising.

C. The "Hard Reality" of Accent AI

1. Gemini Live API: While Gemini 2.0/3.5 has breathtaking speech generation, it is an LLM. It must accumulate phrase tokens before generating audio tokens. You cannot eliminate the 500ms–1s buffer lag on live interactive Zoom calls without violating causality.

2. Open-Source Diffusion Models (Seed-VC / OpenVoice): Excellent in Colab on static WAV files, but on a live microphone loopback they suffer from buffer underruns, clicks, and require a dedicated NVIDIA RTX GPU. Integrated laptop GPUs (Intel Iris Xe) choke, causing runaway latency.

3. RVC Limitation: Retrieval-Based Voice Conversion (RVC) only swaps timbre/pitch, not phonetics. Speaking Indian English through an American RVC model yields an American voice speaking with an Indian accent.
