Comparative engineering evaluation: How Sanas.ai transforms accents at the acoustic signal level (<100ms) without transcribing to text, contrasted against Multimodal LLMs (Gemini Live) and our zero-cost local open-source pipeline (Kokoro-82M + VB-Audio Cable).

Sanas.ai <100ms Formant Shifter Gemini Live S2S (500ms–1.2s) 7-Accent Local Bridge (Free) Zoom / Teams Virtual Mic Zero Cloud Subscription

1. Sanas.ai (Acoustic Filter)

<100ms • Signal DSP

Direct frame-by-frame formant resonance shifting ($F_1, F_2, F_3$). Does NOT transcribe speech to text. Preserves the speaker's original voice timbre, pitch variations, and emotional laugher while neutralizing Indian English phonemes into American or British accents. Lip-sync is perfectly preserved on video calls.

Target: US, UK, AU English Zero Text Transcription Delay

2. Gemini Multimodal Live API

500ms–1.2s • Cloud API

Bidirectional native audio streaming over WebSockets (gemini-2.0-flash-exp). Synthesizes speech tokens directly from input audio tokens. High contextual reasoning, but incurs a 500ms–1.2s buffer lag and completely replaces the speaker's voice with a pre-set bot voice persona (Puck, Kore, Fenrir).

📞 1. The 8kHz Narrowband Telephony Trap

Standard speech models trained on YouTube or podcasts (16kHz–48kHz) fail dramatically on real phone calls:

  • G.711 Compression: Telephone networks cut off all audio above 3,400 Hz and inject line quantization noise.
  • Whisper Looping Trap: OpenAI Whisper's autoregressive decoder gets trapped in infinite repetition loops on telephone hiss (e.g. "पहला वाली...") without VAD suppression.
  • Nemotron Drop Risk: NVIDIA Nemotron dropped over 85% of words because its causal RNN-T expected clean 16kHz studio mics.
  • Bodhan 1.2B Victory: FastConformer trained on Indian ULCA telephony datasets handled 8kHz natively (8.8% WER, 91.2% accuracy).
🎙️ 2. Sanas.ai vs The World (Hard Truth)

Can open-source or LLMs beat Sanas.ai right now? Here is the engineering reality:

  • Signal-Level DSP: Sanas does NOT convert speech to text. It alters acoustic formant resonances ($F_1, F_2, F_3$) directly in audio frames under 80ms–100ms.
  • Why Gemini Live Can't Replace It: Gemini Multimodal Live API accumulates audio tokens in chunks (500ms–1.2s lag) and completely replaces your voice with a bot persona. Video calls suffer lip-sync mismatch and crosstalk.
  • Why RVC Fails: RVC only swaps timbre/pitch; it does NOT neutralize vowels. It outputs an American tone with Indian pronunciation.
⚡ 3. The Working "Jugad" (Local Cascade)

How we solved multi-accent live calls for free in this repository:

  • Engineered Pipeline: Silero VAD (speech-pause trigger) + Faster-Whisper (INT8 quantized) + Multi-TLD TTS engine.
  • Selectable Accents: 7 target accents (US, UK, AU, CA, IE, ZA, IN) selectable at runtime via live_accent_zoom_bridge.py.
  • Virtual Cable Routing: Audio routes natively through VB-Audio Virtual Cable into Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams as a physical mic.
  • Zero Cost: 100% offline, private, runs on laptop CPU or consumer GPU.
🌍 4. Low-Resource & RTL Speech Synthesis

Key findings from our Kurdish (Sorani/Kurmanji) TTS experiments:

  • VITS End-to-End: Meta MMS provides the best lightweight zero-dependency architecture for local server deployment.
  • Flow Matching Naturalness: F5-TTS non-autoregressive flow matching eliminates the robotic jitter common in earlier diffusion models.
  • Script Normalization: Arabic-script Kurdish (Sorani) requires unicode normalization before phonetic tokenization.
