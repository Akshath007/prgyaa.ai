🎙️ Real-Time Accent Translation & Speech-to-Speech (STS) Benchmark

Comparative analysis: Sanas.ai Acoustic Formant Shifting vs. Gemini Live vs. Local Open-Source Cascades.

Open Full Report ↗

1. Sanas.ai (Acoustic Filter)

Direct audio frame-by-frame formant transformation (<100ms latency). Does NOT transcribe to text. Preserves speaker pitch, emotional inflection, and timbre while neutralizing phonemes.

Target: US, UK, AU English

2. Gemini Multimodal Live API

Native Bidirectional Audio over WebSockets. Needs phrase accumulation before outputting speech tokens. High natural prosody, but has 500ms–1.2s buffer delay and replaces user voice with prebuilt bot personas.

Latency: 500ms–1.2s • Cloud API

3. Local Open-Source (Whisper + Kokoro-82M)

Our wired local pipeline: Faster-Whisper + Kokoro-82M / gTTS multi-accent bridge routed into Zoom via VB-Audio Cable. Completely offline, zero subscription cost, with 7 selectable target accents.

7 Accents: US, UK, AU, CA, IE, ZA, IN
